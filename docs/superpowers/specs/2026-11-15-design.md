# Pit Note: paddock field journal

The owner delegated design choices and authorized updating the existing Pages site. Replace the pale shared-card presentation with an independent motorsport editorial: signal red, ivory paper, black timing boards, compressed italic titles, lane stripes and an original schematic. No team marks, racing photography, licensed artwork or implied live telemetry.

Lead with source finding, then manual timezone conversion and a personal notebook. Purpose tabs become a race-briefing panel; source cards become numbered directory rows. Keep all six resources, four paths, checklist, TXT export, reset/cancel behavior, manual offset converter and all caveats. Noindex and relative URLs remain. No network requests, storage or dependencies are added. About page receives the same visual system without changing policy text.

A live scoreboard aesthetic was rejected because the site does not supply live or cached race data. A cinematic photo-heavy landing page was rejected for rights and usability. The selected paddock-journal approach gives the site its own character without overclaiming its capabilities.
